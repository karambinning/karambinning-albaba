Getting here

A short walk from the bus & train stations.

Albaba sits on Kirkgate, the long street that runs from Briggate to Quarry Hill. It's between Leeds Kirkgate Market and the Playhouse — most people walk down from the train station via the markets or via Vicar Lane.

From the train station:
10 mins on foot. Head east through Trinity, down Briggate, then turn right at the bottom onto Kirkgate.

From the bus station:
3 mins on foot. Cross York Street, head west onto Kirkgate.

By car:
Pay-and-display parking on New York Road & in the Q-Park at the Light. The street itself is restricted during the day.

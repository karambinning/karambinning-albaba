The kitchen

Lebanese food at the rough end of Kirkgate — without theatre, without fuss.

Charcoal grills for the shish taouk and lamb. Falafel and kibeh fried to order. The same hummus, tabbouleh and moutabal you'd find on a table in Beirut, set down in foil trays or on plates between the few seats by the window.

Halal, generous, late. Most of the menu can be made vegetarian or vegan. Dine in, take it away, or have it delivered as far as the M62. Whatever you do, order the dip — and the Turkish coffee at the end.

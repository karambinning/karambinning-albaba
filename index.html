The kitchen

A Levantine pantry on a Leeds side-street.

Albaba is a small, family-run Lebanese restaurant on the slow stretch of Kirkgate between the markets and Quarry Hill. The kitchen runs charcoal grills for the kebabs and the shish tawouk, deep-fries falafel to order, and turns out the same tabbouleh, hummus and labneh you'd find on a table in Beirut — without fuss, without theatre, without trying to be anything other than what it is.

Almost everything on the menu can be made vegetarian or vegan. All meat is halal. Dine in at one of the tables on the ground floor, take it away in a foil tray, or have it delivered as far as the ring road. Whatever you do, order the dip — and the Turkish coffee at the end.

What the regulars order

Six dishes worth a walk down Kirkgate.

  1. 01

    Falafel Wrap

    Chickpea-and-herb croquettes fried to order, rolled into warm flatbread with tahini, pickle and parsley. The single most-ordered thing in the kitchen.

  2. 02

    Shish Tawouk

    Chicken thigh marinated in garlic, lemon and yoghurt, skewered and finished over the charcoal grill until it's crisp at the edges and slipping off the skewer.

  3. 03

    Hummus & Bread

    Chickpea purée with sesame paste, lemon and a slick of olive oil. Comes with a stack of warm bread for tearing. Worth bringing friends for.

  4. 04

    Lentil Soup

    Red-lentil soup with cumin, lemon and a swirl of olive oil — what the office workers from the city centre come in for in winter.

  5. 05

    Mixed Grill

    Shish, kofta and shish tawouk on one platter, served with rice, salad, pickled chilli and a dish of garlic sauce. Enough for two if you don't mind sharing.

  6. 06

    Turkish Coffee

    Brewed slow in a long-handled pot, served thick, dark and unfiltered. The way to end the meal at one in the morning.

Find us

29 Kirkgate. Five minutes from the bus station.

We sit between Leeds Kirkgate Market and Quarry Hill on the south side of the city centre. The 11 bus drops you outside; the train station is a ten-minute walk if you cut through the arcades.

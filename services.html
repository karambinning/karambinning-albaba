The menu

Mezze, grill,
coffee, repeat.

A brief tour of what comes out of the kitchen. Almost everything can be made vegetarian or vegan — just ask. All meat is halal. Prices and exact specials change with what the kitchen has in; ring or call in for today's board.

Mezze · Cold

From the cold counter. Mostly vegan, all served with warm flatbread.

  • Hummus Chickpea purée, tahini, lemon, olive oil
  • Baba Ghanoush Smoked aubergine, tahini, garlic, lemon
  • Moutabal Char-grilled aubergine, yoghurt, tahini
  • Tabbouleh Parsley, bulgur, tomato, lemon, mint
  • Fattoush Mixed leaves, sumac, crispy bread, pomegranate
  • Labneh Strained yoghurt, mint, olive oil
  • Hummus Beiruti Hummus with chilli, parsley and garlic
  • Olives & Pickles House mix of olives and Lebanese pickles

Mezze · Hot

Fried, baked or grilled to order. Best at the start of a long meal.

  • Falafel Chickpea-and-herb croquettes, tahini sauce
  • Kibbeh Bulgur shells stuffed with spiced lamb and pine nuts
  • Sambousek Hot pastries with cheese, spinach or meat
  • Halloumi Pan-fried halloumi, lemon
  • Foul Medames Slow-cooked broad beans, garlic, lemon
  • Cheese Rolls Crisp filo with melted cheese
  • Spicy Potatoes Crisped potatoes, coriander, garlic, chilli
  • Makanek Lebanese sausage, pomegranate molasses

Soups & Salads

Small bowls, big bowls — what office workers come in for at lunch.

  • Lentil Soup Red lentil, cumin, lemon, olive oil
  • Soup of the Day Ask at the counter
  • Greek Salad Feta, tomato, cucumber, olives, oregano
  • Mixed Leaves Sumac, lemon dressing

From the Charcoal Grill

Skewers and platters — served with rice, salad and garlic sauce.

  • Shish Tawouk Marinated chicken thigh, lemon, garlic, yoghurt
  • Lamb Shish Cubes of lamb, charred at the edges
  • Kofta Skewers Minced lamb, parsley, onion, spice
  • Mixed Grill Shish, kofta and tawouk on one platter
  • Chicken Wings Marinated and charred over coals
  • Lamb Chops Grilled simply, served pink
  • Half Roast Chicken From the rotisserie
  • Vegetable Skewers Courgette, pepper, onion, halloumi

Wraps & Sandwiches

Rolled in warm flatbread. The lunchtime queue's whole reason for existing.

  • Falafel Wrap Falafel, tahini, salad, pickles
  • Shawarma Chicken Marinated chicken, garlic, salad
  • Shawarma Lamb Slow-roast lamb, tahini, parsley
  • Halloumi Wrap Grilled halloumi, salad, chilli
  • Shish Tawouk Wrap Skewered chicken, garlic, lettuce
  • Kofta Wrap Grilled kofta, onion, parsley

From the oven and the fryer. Manakish are baked to order.

  • Manakish Za'atar Flatbread baked with za'atar and olive oil
  • Manakish Cheese Flatbread baked with akkawi cheese
  • Manakish Meat Lamb mince, onion, spice

Desserts & Drinks

The end of the meal. The Turkish coffee is the move.

  • Baklava Layered filo, pistachio, syrup
  • Knafeh Cheese pastry in rosewater syrup
  • Lebanese Ice Cream Mastic and rose, pistachio crust
  • Mhalabieh Milk pudding with orange blossom
  • Turkish Coffee Brewed in a long-handled pot, thick and dark
  • Lebanese Tea Black tea, mint or sage on request
  • Fresh Juices Orange, lemonade, mint
  • Soft Drinks Plus Lebanese sparkling water

Menu items are a representative selection of what the kitchen serves regularly. Specials and seasonal additions appear on the chalkboard. Ring the kitchen for tonight's full list or to ask after a specific dish — staff are happy to talk you through it.

Ring ahead — or just turn up.

The kitchen runs from lunch through to late. The grill takes a few minutes; the mezze comes straight out.

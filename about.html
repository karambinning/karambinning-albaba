About the kitchen

A Lebanese kitchen
on a Leeds side-street.

No claims to authenticity, no manifesto on the wall — just the same hummus, falafel and shish that have been keeping the late-night crowd, the lunch crowd and the office crowd happy on Kirkgate for years.

Albaba sits on the long, slightly tatty end of Kirkgate that runs from Leeds Kirkgate Market down towards Quarry Hill. It's the kind of street most of the city only passes through, which is how a small Lebanese kitchen with a charcoal grill in the back ended up being one of the quietest secrets in the city centre — a place people from offices in Quarry Hill, students cutting back from a night out, and taxi drivers on a break all know.

The kitchen is small. The menu is long. Mezze comes from the cold counter and the fryer — hummus, baba ghanoush, tabbouleh, falafel, kibbeh, sambousek — and the grill in the back does the kebabs, the shish tawouk and the mixed grills to order. Bread comes warm. The lentil soup is made fresh. The Turkish coffee is brewed in a long-handled pot, one cup at a time.

"Why don't you try our dip — or our Turkish coffee?"

Everything that can be vegetarian or vegan, is. All meat is halal. Prices are reasonable in a way that is genuinely rare for the centre of Leeds in 2026. Dine in at one of the tables on the ground floor, take it away wrapped in foil, or order delivery as far as the ring road via the usual apps. Whichever way you do it, the kitchen is open from late morning right through to the small hours.

Albaba doesn't have a story written in PR copy. The people behind the counter cook food they grew up with, for a city that mostly lives on takeaway pizza and chain noodles. That, honestly, is the whole pitch.

Worth knowing

A few quick facts.

Cuisine

Lebanese · Mediterranean · Middle Eastern. Charcoal grill in the back.

Halal

All meat is halal-certified. Sourced from suppliers serving Leeds' Lebanese community.

Vegetarian & vegan

Most mezze are naturally vegan. The kitchen will adapt the wraps, the salads and most of the mains on request.

Eat-in & takeaway

Sit-down service on the ground floor. Foil-tray takeaway through the same counter. Delivery via the major apps.

Late kitchen

The grill stays on past midnight Friday and Saturday, until 1:40 am — long after most kitchens in the centre have closed.

Family friendly

Small groups, large groups, kids, lunch meetings, late suppers — all welcome. Generally quieter at lunch.

Easiest way to find out is turn up.

Or ring ahead and we'll have a table set, an order packed, or the grill on for you.
